PRIVACY POLICY — Evolver Evolver is local-first: quests, meals, photos, and Life Bio stay on your phone. Below is what leaves the device only after you opt in. ON DEVICE Profile, XP/Aura, tasks, food journal, milestones, and .zip backups stay local (SQLite / files). Android Auto Backup may include app data unless you disable it in system settings. ELO AI With your consent, Elo AI (chat, meal analysis, and long-term quest estimates) sends context (Life Bio, goals, meal text/photo, messages) for processing via the Google Gemini API. Without consent, Elo AI calls are not sent. LEADERBOARD AND AVATAR With consent, Supabase sync sends aggregates (level, XP, streak, task counts, health score, username, frame) and may upload a public avatar. Quest titles, meal names, and meal photos never go to the cloud. You can turn sync off or hide nutrition details in Settings. YOUR CONTROLS Change consent in Profile Settings. Turning off Elo AI or the leaderboard stops new uploads immediately. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes the local account on this device. For privacy questions, contact the app developer.
