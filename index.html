EVOLVER · LEGAL

Privacy Policy

Evolver is local-first: quests, meals, photos, and Life Bio stay on your phone. Below is what leaves the device only after you opt in.

On Device

Profile, XP/Aura, tasks, food journal, milestones, and .zip backups stay local (SQLite / files). Android Auto Backup may include app data unless you disable it in system settings.

Elo AI

With your consent, Elo AI (chat, meal analysis, and long-term quest estimates) sends context (Life Bio, goals, meal text/photo, messages) for processing via the Google Gemini API. Without consent, Elo AI calls are not sent.

Leaderboard and Avatar

With consent, Supabase sync sends aggregates (level, XP, streak, task counts, health score, username, frame) and may upload a public avatar. Quest titles, meal names, and meal photos never go to the cloud. You can turn sync off or hide nutrition details in Settings.

Your Controls

Change consent in Profile Settings. Turning off Elo AI or the leaderboard stops new uploads immediately. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes the local account on this device.

For privacy questions, contact the app developer at stefanv2233@gmail.com.
EVOLVER · PRIVATNOST

Politika privatnosti

Evolver je local-first: questovi, obroci, fotografije i Life Bio ostaju na telefonu. Ispod je šta deliš tek kad eksplicitno pristaneš.

Na uređaju

Profil, XP/Aura, zadaci, food journal, milestone-i i rezervne .zip kopije čuvaju se lokalno (SQLite / fajlovi). Backup može da uključi Android Auto Backup osim ako ga isključiš u sistemu.

Elo AI

Uz tvoju saglasnost, Elo AI (chat, analiza obroka i procena dugoročnih zadataka) šalje kontekst (Life Bio, ciljevi, opis/foto obroka, poruke) na obradu preko Google Gemini API-ja. Bez saglasnosti Elo AI pozivi se ne šalju.

Rang lista i avatar

Uz saglasnost, sync na Supabase šalje agregate (nivo, XP, streak, broj taskova, health score, username, frame) i po želji javni avatar. Naslovi zadataka, imena jela i meal photo ne idu u cloud. Možeš isključiti sync ili sakriti nutrition detalje u Podešavanjima.

Tvoja kontrola

Saglasnost menjaš u Podešavanjima profila. Isključivanje Elo AI / rang liste odmah zaustavlja nova slanja. Brisanje app podataka ili deinstalacija briše lokalni nalog na uređaju.

Za pitanja o privatnosti kontaktiraj developera aplikacije na TVOJ_EMAIL@gmail.com.
